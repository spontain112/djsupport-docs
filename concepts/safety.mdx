DJ Support separates inspection, Spotify mutation, and Approval. Each requires distinct authority.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What stays local?" icon="hard-drive">
    OAuth credentials, source-library data, matching knowledge, Corrections, Transfer manifests, playlist state, and reports stay in private operating-system application storage.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Preview change Spotify?" icon="eye">
    No. Preview can perform matching and retain a resumable checkpoint, but it cannot create or update Spotify playlists.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can an AI assistant approve for me?" icon="bot">
    No. Conversation is never authorization. DJ Support does not infer Approval, Corrections, source relinking, or destructive playlist intent.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Begin with one selected playlist. Whole-library processing is deliberately explicit because it can be expensive.
</Tip>
