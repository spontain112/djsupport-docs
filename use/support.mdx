DJ Support is free, open-source, and self-supported software. There is no guaranteed support, response time, compatibility commitment, or recovery service.

The documentation and your local AI agent should handle ordinary setup and troubleshooting. You may open a reproducible bug or feature request in the public GitHub tracker, but the maintainer is not obligated to respond or provide individual help.

## Never include private user data

Do not paste or attach:

- Spotify credentials or tokens;
- Rekordbox library or audio paths;
- playlist or track contents;
- reports, Corrections, Approved Matches, or matching state;
- backups or application-data files;
- Spotify account identifiers.

Prefer the DJ Support version, macOS version, command name, redacted reason code, and the smallest synthetic reproduction.
