Preview is evidence, not permission. Continue one explicit decision at a time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Qualification Workspace">
    For a selected Rekordbox playlist, compare source facts with the proposed Spotify matches. If you authorized Local Audition, you can hear the exact selected source occurrence locally.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Qualify each item needing attention">
    Choose **Correct**, **Wrong — find another**, **Cannot verify**, or **Not my source**. These choices form a private Qualification Draft; they are not Approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the Spotify write">
    Applying the Qualification Draft and publishing the Provisional Playlist changes Spotify. Your Agent Client must ask for explicit Spotify-write authorization for this bounded action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Provisional Playlist in Spotify">
    Listen in Spotify. Removing a proposed track before Approval rejects that proposed match. A published playlist still carries no matching authority.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve separately">
    Approval compares the reviewed playlist with its Publication Manifest. Surviving or corrected matches become Approved Matches; removed proposals become Rejected Matches. Approval itself performs no additional Spotify mutation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Conversation, a match score, Preview, Qualification, and playlist publication are never Approval.
</Warning>

## When a proposal is wrong

- Use **Wrong — find another** to enter the bounded Correction workflow.
- Choose the Spotify result yourself; searching does not create a Correction.
- Use **Cannot verify** when you need to stop without inventing a verdict.
- Never put private playlist contents, file paths, or reports into a GitHub issue.
